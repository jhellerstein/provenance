\section{Open Questions}
\label{app:open-questions}

\paragraph{Universality of the determination semiring.}
Green et al.~\cite{green2007provenance} identify $\mathbb{N}[X]$ as the free
(universal) object for positive-RA provenance. The determination semiring adds
a filtration; is there a universal filtered object through which every
adequate consumer factors? A positive answer would justify ``algebra'' in the
strong sense and give a canonical normal form for determination provenance.

\paragraph{Dynamic commutativity and canonical layering.}
In both instantiations here commutativity is state-independent, so the layer
structure of a trace is unambiguous. In general, whether two commitments
commute may depend on the current residual, and canonical layering becomes
subtle; characterizing when a canonical filtration still exists (and when
competing sound factorizations must be compared instead) is open.

\paragraph{Quantitative negation beyond positivity.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:monus-elimination} matches sealing and monus on
\emph{supports} under zero-divisor-free, $\omega$-continuous semirings. When
$K$ has zero divisors (e.g.\ the bounded lattice semiring
$(\{0,\dots,m\}, \max, \min, 0, m)$), monus can assign an intermediate value
$1_K \mathbin{\dot{-}} v \notin \{0_K, 1_K\}$, a quantitative residual that
sealing discards. Whether graded commitments can record \emph{how much} of a
derivation a negation overcomes---recovering this residual within the
determination framework---is open.

\paragraph{A joint responsibility game.}
Section~\ref{sec:responsibility} attributes contingency to commitments and
tuple-level responsibility~\cite{meliou2010causality} attributes derivation to
data tuples; the two are sequential. Unifying them into a single game whose
players are both commitments and tuples---and understanding its Shapley
value---remains open.

\paragraph{Depth reduction.}
A coarser output order $\OrdO' \supseteq \OrdO$ makes some layer's choices
$\OrdO'$-comparable, eliminating that layer and lowering depth. Characterizing
the minimal depth achievable by order coarsening (and its cost in lost
distinctions) is open.
