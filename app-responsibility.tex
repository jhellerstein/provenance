\section{The Shapley Refinement of Responsibility}
\label{app:responsibility-details}

This appendix is self-contained and may be read, or omitted, independently of
the body. Section~\ref{sec:responsibility} defined the responsibility value
$\operatorname{resp}_a(e)$ as the density of a commitment's pivotal set and
noted that it refines, when an application supplies a value function on
coalitions of commitments, to a Shapley value. We develop that refinement here:
its definition, $\#\mathrm{P}$-hardness, bounded-treewidth tractability, an
approximation, and the per-layer form for depth $>1$. None of the body's
results depend on this material.

\subsection{Definition}

Fix an observable $a$, a resolving determination $D^\ast$, and treat each
commitment occurrence in $D^\ast$ as a player. For a coalition $C$ of
commitments, let $v(C)$ be the probability (under the product measure on the
remaining commitments) that $a$ holds when the commitments in $C$ are fixed to
their $D^\ast$-values and the rest are drawn at random. The \emph{Shapley
responsibility} of $\varphi_i$ is its average marginal contribution
$v(C \cup \{i\}) - v(C)$ over all orderings---the standard Shapley
value~\cite{shapley1953value} of this presence game. It is consistent with
Definition~\ref{def:responsibility}: $\operatorname{resp}_a(e)$ is the
expected pivotality of $e$, and the Shapley value distributes total
contingency across commitments.

\subsection{Hardness}

\begin{theorem}[\#P-hardness]
  \label{thm:resp-hard}
  Computing the Shapley responsibility of a commitment is \#P-hard, already
  for single-layer determinations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
  We follow the Shapley-in-databases
  reduction~\cite{livshits2021shapley}. The marginal
  $v(\{i\}) - v(\emptyset)$ requires counting the satisfying assignments of
  the support formula with $x_i$ fixed; for monotone support formulas this is
  \#P-hard by the hardness of counting monotone-DNF models (equivalently,
  weighted model counting over tuple-independent lineage). The gadget uses $n$
  independent binary commitments whose support formula is the given monotone
  DNF.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Tractability at Bounded Treewidth}

\begin{theorem}[FPT in support treewidth]
  \label{thm:resp-tw}
  For a single-layer determination whose monotone support formula has
  primal-graph treewidth $w$, Shapley responsibility is computable in
  $O(2^w \cdot n^2 \cdot \mathrm{poly}(n))$---FPT in $w$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
  Each $v(C)$ is a weighted model count of the support formula with the
  variables in $C$ fixed; by Theorem~\ref{thm:treewidth-app} this is
  $O(2^w \cdot \mathrm{poly}(n))$. The Shapley value aggregates
  $O(n^2)$ such counts (one per coalition size and player), giving the bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Robustness is FPT in support treewidth]
  \label{cor:robustness-fpt}
  Under the hypothesis of Theorem~\ref{thm:resp-tw}, robustness and support
  density are computable in $O(2^w \cdot \mathrm{poly}(n))$
  (Theorem~\ref{thm:treewidth-app}).
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Approximation}

\begin{proposition}[Additive approximation]
  \label{prop:resp-approx}
  Shapley responsibility admits an additive $\varepsilon$-approximation with
  confidence $1-\delta$ in $O(\varepsilon^{-2} \log \delta^{-1})$ samples by
  permutation sampling~\cite{shapley1953value,livshits2021shapley}: each
  sample draws a random order, fixes the preceding players to their
  $D^\ast$-values, completes the rest at random, and records
  $\varphi_i$'s marginal.
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Multi-Layer Responsibility}
\label{app:multi-layer-resp}

For depth $d > 1$, responsibility is defined \emph{per layer}: the
responsibility of a commitment at layer $k$ is its Shapley value in the
presence game conditioned on layers $1, \dots, k{-}1$ being discharged. This
respects non-commutativity---one never asks ``what if a layer-$2$ commitment
preceded a layer-$1$ commitment,'' which the layer structure forbids---and
each layer's game is the single-layer game of the preceding subsections over
the cell fixed by the discharged prefix. We deliberately do \emph{not} claim
that responsibility concentrates monotonically under joins: Shapley values are
not monotone under support containment, so a join can redistribute rather than
concentrate responsibility.
