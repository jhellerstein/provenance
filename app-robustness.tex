\section{Robustness and Treewidth}
\label{app:robustness-proofs}

\subsection{Robustness is coNP-complete}

\begin{theorem}[Robustness is coNP-complete in width]
  \label{thm:robustness-conp}
  Consider any instantiation in which the relevant commitment layer has $n$
  independent binary commitments, validity checking is polynomial, and query
  evaluation is polynomial. Deciding robustness ($Y_a = \Dets$) is
  coNP-complete.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  Membership: $a$ is \emph{non}-robust iff some determination makes $a$ false,
  a polynomial-size certificate (the layer's commitment choices) checkable in
  polynomial time by the two conditions. Hardness is by reduction from
  \textsc{DNF-Validity} (coNP-complete): given
  $\psi = t_1 \vee \cdots \vee t_\ell$ over $x_1, \dots, x_n$, use $n$
  independent binary commitments, one per variable, so $\Dets$ has $2^n$
  elements, one per assignment $\alpha_D$. Build a UCQ
  $Q = Q_1 \cup \cdots \cup Q_\ell$ whose distinguished tuple $t^\ast$ is
  selected under $D$ iff $\alpha_D$ satisfies some term of $\psi$. Then
  $t^\ast$ is robust iff $\psi$ is valid.
\end{proof}

\noindent
In the $\mbox{Datalog}^\neg$ instantiation this coincides with the known
coNP-completeness of skeptical reasoning under stable-model
semantics~\cite{marek1991autoepistemic}: robustness ``$Y_a = \Dets$'' is
exactly ``$a$ holds in every stable model.'' In the transactional
instantiation the same gadget uses $n$ write--write pairs
(Appendix~\ref{sec:transactions}).

\subsection{Bounded-Treewidth Tractability}

We restate Theorem~\ref{thm:treewidth} and make the locality condition
precise. Fix a provenance query $Q$, an output observable $a$, and its
single-layer support formula $\phi_a$ over commitment variables, obtained from
base supports by the homomorphism of Proposition~\ref{prop:support-hom}
($\cup \mapsto \vee$, $\cap \mapsto \wedge$).

\begin{theorem}[Bounded-treewidth support and robustness]
  \label{thm:treewidth-app}
  If the primal graph of $\phi_a$ has treewidth $w$, then the why-support of
  $a$---hence robustness and impossibility---is decidable in
  $O(2^w \cdot \mathrm{poly}(n))$, and under a product measure on commitment
  variables the support density $\mu(Y_a)$ is computable in the same time.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  $\phi_a$ is a monotone Boolean combination of the base variables; validity,
  satisfiability, and weighted model counting over a formula of primal-graph
  treewidth $w$ run in $O(2^w \cdot \mathrm{poly}(n))$ by dynamic programming
  on a width-$w$ tree decomposition. Robustness is validity of $\phi_a$;
  impossibility is unsatisfiability; density is the weighted model count.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Locality]
  \label{def:locality}
  A workload is \emph{local} if, for every output observable, each clause of
  its support formula involves only commitments that share a
  dependency/conflict edge (equivalently, the support formula introduces no
  primal-graph edge absent from the conflict/dependency graph).
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Conflict treewidth suffices under locality]
  \label{prop:locality}
  For a local workload, the primal-graph treewidth of every output's support
  formula is at most the treewidth of the conflict/dependency graph.
  Consequently Theorem~\ref{thm:treewidth-app} holds with $w$ the conflict
  treewidth.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
  Locality makes the primal graph of each support formula a subgraph of the
  conflict/dependency graph, and treewidth is monotone under subgraphs.
\end{proof}

\noindent
Locality is why we state Theorem~\ref{thm:treewidth} over the \emph{support
formula}'s treewidth rather than the conflict graph's: in general transitive
ordering constraints can add primal-graph edges absent from the conflict
graph, so the two can differ; under locality they agree, and both
instantiations' natural workloads (bounded-degree negative dependency,
bounded-contention conflict) are local.
