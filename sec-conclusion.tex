\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Determination provenance makes the commitments that resolve semantic ambiguity
explicit and composable. Provenance over the complete determination space
forms a product semiring $K^{\Dets}$ whose Boolean shadow---supports---carries
a canonical layer-prefix filtration; the filtration measures support depth, is
respected by provenance composition, and recovers classical provenance (and
the Green--Karvounarakis--Tannen relational semiring) as the result-extensional
case at depth $0$. $\mbox{Datalog}^\neg$ instantiates the framework so that the
classical negation semantics are different reading depths of one shared
filtration; transactional isolation (Appendix~\ref{sec:transactions})
instantiates it in a very different domain, where the same provenance queries
compare a tuple across isolation levels and certify when a query result is
isolation-insensitive---sharpened by the fact that serializability and snapshot
isolation are incomparable in support depth. The filtration supports quantitative diagnosis---work regret, semantic
shift, commitment responsibility---and a bounded-treewidth algorithm for
support and robustness, going beyond binary robustness to explain \emph{how
much} and \emph{why} an observable depends on each layer of resolution.

The recipe is uniform: given a system's event trace, declare an outcome space
and refinement order that identify the commitments, supply an adequate
provenance consumer, and the algebra applies---robustness, why/why-not,
responsibility, cross-specification comparison---over existing observability.

Looking ahead, the determination semiring already has the shape of a
probability space: $\Dets$ is a sample space, supports are measurable sets,
and the canonical filtration is a filtration in the measure-theoretic sense.
Under a measure on $\Dets$, support densities become probabilities, work
regret becomes expected regret, and responsibility becomes conditional
expectation. That the algebra lands so close to the machinery of stochastic
processes suggests a deeper connection worth developing.
